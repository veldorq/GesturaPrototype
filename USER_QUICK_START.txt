================================
  HAND GESTURE CONTROL
================================

🚀 INSTANT START

Double-click:
  HandGestureControl.exe

That's it! No installation needed.

================================

🎮 BASIC GESTURES

Scrolling:
  ✋ Open palm → Scroll down
  ✊ Closed fist → Scroll up

Mouse:
  ☝️ Index finger → Move cursor
  🖖 Three fingers → Click

Exit:
  Press Q on keyboard

================================

💡 FIRST TIME TIPS

1. Keep hand visible in camera
2. Plain background works best
3. Good lighting helps
4. Practice for 2 minutes
5. Make clear gestures

================================

📋 WHAT YOU NEED

✓ Windows 10 or 11
✓ Any webcam
✓ Nothing else!

================================

🐛 NOT WORKING?

Camera issues:
  - Allow camera in Windows settings
  - Close other apps using camera
  - Restart the app

Gestures not detected:
  - Improve lighting
  - Move closer/farther from camera
  - Make gestures more obvious

Still stuck?
  Try restarting your computer

================================

🎯 ADVANCED GESTURES

Want more control?

Four fingers → Refresh page
Thumb up → Zoom in
Peace sign ✌️ → Click
Swipe left/right → Back/Forward
Rock sign 🤘 (hold 1.5s) → Exit app

The app shows these hints in
the camera window!
